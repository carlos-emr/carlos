### S25-P2: Port-conflict consent is given for the listed services, but the stop step re-scans and stops whatever qualifies at that moment
- Severity: Low
- Category: bug
- Confidence: Confirmed (mechanism); the race window is narrow
- Commit: pre-existing (#5439/#5440, main); free_front_door edited by 051ed276
- Location:
  - `debian/carlos-emr.postinst:258` (plan) and `:276` (execute); same lines on main
  - `debian/assets/bin/carlos-emr-host-conflicts:302-311,350-372`
- Problem: The debconf question lists the `--plan` output. After the answer, `host_conflicts front-door "${front_door_ip}"` runs without the approved list. `front_door()` calls `colliding` again and stops and disables every qualifying unit it now finds. A service that binds 80/443 while the prompt is open is stopped and disabled without having been shown; the prompt can sit open indefinitely. The same pattern is in carlos-ctl `provision._free_front_door` (another repository).
- Evidence: postinst:258 `front_door_plan="$(host_conflicts front-door --plan …)"`, then :276 `FRONT_DOOR_FREED="$(host_conflicts front-door "${front_door_ip}" …)"`. In the helper, free mode is `found="$(colliding …)"`, then `stop_unit "${unit}"` for each row, with no list argument.
- Impact: Rare. When it happens, a service the operator never approved is stopped and kept off at boot. After 051ed276 made consent explicit, this matters more.
- Correction: Pass the approved unit list to the free mode, stop only units in both the plan and the fresh scan, and report any others as blocked.
- Pre-existing: yes (present on origin/main)
