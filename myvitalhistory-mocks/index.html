MyVitalHistory

Your health records, kept by you

MyVitalHistory stores the letters, results, and reports your clinic sends you, so you always have your own copy — even if you change doctors.

Only you can open this Everything is scrambled with a passphrase that only you know. Your clinic can't read it, and neither can we.
Works without internet Your records are kept on this device. You don't need a connection to open them.
Onboarding, step 2. Passphrase, not password — length beats complexity and it is easier to say out loud. The warning is deliberately blunt because there is no reset.
MyVitalHistory — Setup

Choose your passphrase

Use three or four random words you'll remember. Longer is safer than complicated.

There is no "forgot passphrase" Because nobody else holds your key, we can't reset it for you. If you forget it and have no recovery method, your records can't be opened again. We'll help you set up a backup way in on the next screen.
Onboarding, step 3 — the open design question (#3474). Three ways to survive a forgotten passphrase, each shifting risk somewhere different. Mocked so the trade-off can be argued about concretely; not a decision.

Security & backup

How your records are protected and copied

Encryption On — always
Your records are scrambled on this device using your passphrase. This can't be turned off.
Recovery sheet Printed 2 years ago
Your printed sheet still works. If you've lost it, print a new one — the old sheet stops working straight away.
Backup to iCloud On
Last backed up 20 minutes ago. Your records are copied to iCloud still scrambled — Apple can't read them, and neither can anyone who gets into your iCloud account.
Backup to a folder or drive Off
Keep a second copy on a USB stick or a folder you choose. Recommended if your records matter to you — cloud storage can fill up or expire.
Lock automatically
After 15 minutes without use.
Unlock with Touch ID On
Your passphrase is still needed after restarting, and once a week.
Change passphrase
Re-locks every record with the new one. Takes a few minutes for a large vault.
