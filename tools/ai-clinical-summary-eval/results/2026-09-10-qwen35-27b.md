## Additional validation and defects found

- A new renal-monitoring case passed 1/1 with 1.000 recall and no findings.
- A new thyroid-adjustment case produced all expected, quotation-grounded facts,
  but exposed host precedence that classified `TSH ... increased` as a
  medication action. Results now take precedence over generic change verbs;
  replaying the preserved response through current code passes all gates.
- Qwen 3.5 2B is not a safe substitute for the broad delta task. It omitted
  lab/medication facts and sometimes emitted malformed JSON despite the schema.
  It remains useful only for narrow screening.
- Retaining 27B between rows left about 1.2 GiB available and caused the Ollama
  worker to exit with EOF while updating its prompt cache after three consecutive
  requests. Every campaign request now sets `keep_alive: 0`. A live 2B check and
  the nine-row 27B checkpoint/stability sequence confirmed that each worker
  unloads after its response and memory returns to about 18 GiB available.

## Decision

The full-summary contract remains rejected. The normalized, filtered,
quotation-backed delta is the only eligible 27B candidate in these tests. It is
ready for broader synthetic and expert review, but the small invented case set
does not establish clinical safety or justify production promotion on its own.
